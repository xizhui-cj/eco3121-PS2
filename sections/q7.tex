\noindent (b) Holding education constant, a one-unit increase in ability
is associated with 2.086 more units of hourly wage ($\hat{\beta}_2=2.086$).
One additional year of education is associated with 0.512 more units of
ability ($\tilde{\delta}_1=0.512$).

Their product is 1.069 wage units per year of
education. Omitting ability attributes its wage association to education
to the extent that the two variables are correlated. This confounding
term accounts for the increase from $\hat{\beta}_1=3.690$ to
$\tilde{\beta}_1=4.760$.
